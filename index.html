Why Join

Welcoming & Friendly

Whether you’re slipping into a new realm through a book, tackling a few game quests, or resting between adventures, Odyssey Tavern is your safe place to land.

Amazing Books

Looking for a new world? We choose our next read through weekly or monthly book polls — it all depends on the size of the book and how quickly everyone feels comfortable reading. No pressure and no rush.

Gaming

Ready for an adventure? Life sims, RPGs, dungeon crawlers, FPS — whatever you’re into, you don’t have to roam solo. Odyssey Tavern is full of travelers excited to explore new realms together.

Fun Events

From book polls to game nights, realm challenges to guild hangouts, Odyssey Tavern always has something brewing. Join in when you want, skip when you need — the tavern is always open.

Events

Choose Our Next Realm!

Every traveler reads at their own pace, so our book polls shift with the journey. Every two weeks or on the first Monday of the month, we vote on our next realm to explore — thick books, short books, cozy reads, chaotic adventures, whatever the tavern chooses.

Bard's In!

Each evening, the Bard brings music and stories to the tavern. Come solo or with your guild and share your latest adventures.

Choose Our Next Adventure!

Whenever we finish a game, the tavern chooses the next one. New worlds, new quests, new fun.

Tavern Night!

Gather every Friday with fellow travelers for our movie, show, or anime stream.
